CustomerCare AI Assistant is an AI-powered email management and customer support automation system that streamlines communication workflows and improves response efficiency. The platform automatically fetches incoming emails and classifies them into categories such as Support, Sales, Complaints, Billing, and General Inquiries using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques. VaultQuery — Knowledge-Grounded Q&A System

VaultQuery is an AI-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system designed to provide accurate, context-aware answers from document-based knowledge sources. Built using LangChain, the platform retrieves relevant information from uploaded documents and combines it with large language models to generate reliable, evidence-based responses. By grounding answers in retrieved context, VaultQuery significantly improves response accuracy, reduces hallucinations, and ensures users receive information directly supported by the source material. The system is ideal for enterprise knowledge management, document search, research assistance, and intelligent information retrieval.
